About

We work at the intersection of infectious diseases and clinical pharmacology, with the goal of improving how anti-infective drugs are selected, dosed, and monitored. This site collects the open code, data, and analysis pipelines behind our research.

The lab is led by Russell E. Lewis, Associate Professor of Infectious Diseases in the Department of Molecular Medicine at the University of Padua.

Research areas

Antimicrobial PK/PD

Pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic modelling to support rational dosing of anti-infectives.

Clinical infectious diseases

Clinical studies of diagnosis, treatment, and outcomes in serious infections.

Reproducible research

Open, reusable code and data so our analyses can be checked and built upon.
